## Company Jobs Feed (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) Aggregates and normalizes open job listings from company career sites that use Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or SmartRecruiters into a single schema. Reads the public job board APIs published by those ATS platforms and returns structured job metadata including availability state (open/closed), platform and company identifiers, job identifiers and organizational details (title, department, team), location and country with a remote indicator and workplace type, employment type, posting date, salary ranges and currency (from structured fields or detected in text), job page and application links, optional plain-text job descriptions, and timestamps for when jobs were first seen and when closures are detected. Supports filter options for title keywords, locations, remote-only, and recent postings, can process large lists of companies with each site read independently, and offers a monitor mode that delivers only new jobs and can report jobs that have disappeared. ## Use cases - Sales prospecting: find accounts actively hiring for roles that signal product fit (e.g., data engineers). - Recruiting and staffing: discover remote engineering and other target-role openings from specific companies. - Job boards and aggregators: ingest fresh jobs with descriptions and pay ranges for listing or indexing. - Investors and market research: daily hiring tracker that reports new hires and detects closed/removed job postings. - Salary benchmarking: compile city- or region-specific job listings with extracted or detected pay ranges.

Fonte dos dados: boards-api.greenhouse.io, api.lever.co, api.ashbyhq.com, api.smartrecruiters.com.
