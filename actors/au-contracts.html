## Australian Government Contracts (AusTender Contract Notices) This Actor reads the official AusTender contract notices (Commonwealth contract awards) and extracts structured contract-award data for Australian government procurement: agency and supplier details including ABNs and location, contract values and currency, award and contract date ranges, procurement method and classification codes (e.g., UNSPSC), amendment indicators, and links to the original notices. It produces clean, table-friendly structured outputs (JSON/CSV/Excel) suitable for analysis, reporting and automated monitoring, and can be run on a schedule to surface new contract notices. ## Use cases - Competitor tracking: monitor named suppliers or ABNs to detect awarded contracts and contract value changes. - Sales prospecting: find potential customers by searching contract descriptions and procurement categories for relevant keywords. - Account planning: analyze purchasing patterns of specific agencies to identify opportunities and suppliers. - Market sizing and spend analysis: aggregate contract awards by UNSPSC/family, agency, time period or value bands to quantify market opportunity. - Ongoing monitoring: scheduled runs to capture only newly published contract notices for timely alerts.

Fonte dos dados: https://api.tenders.gov.au/ocds/findByDates/contractPublished.
