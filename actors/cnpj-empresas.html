## Consulta CNPJ em Lote (Receita Federal) Bulk lookup of Brazilian company registrations (CNPJ) that retrieves official cadastral records from the Federal Revenue open data and public services, producing structured company registration data for each CNPJ: legal and trade names, registration status with date, main and secondary activity codes with descriptions (CNAE), legal nature and size, share capital, tax regime history and Simples/MEI options, full address components (street, number, neighborhood, city, state, postal code, IBGE code), telephone and corporate email when available, corporate shareholder entities and total shareholder count. The Actor queries open CNPJ datasets via public services (primary source and fallback), returns structured records suitable for bulk processing and export (JSON/CSV/Excel), identifies invalid or non-existent identifiers separately, and applies LGPD-aware filtering (omits personal shareholder details; omits identifying data for sole proprietors/MEI; discards personal email providers). ## Use cases - Bulk CNPJ lookup for large lists of companies - Validate customer and supplier registrations (KYB) - Enrich CRM/company lists with official registration and activity data - Check company registration status before issuing invoices - Analyze and audit portfolios of companies

Fonte dos dados: https://minhareceita.org, https://brasilapi.com.br/api/cnpj/v1.
