## Shopify Store Monitor (Prices, Stock & New Products) An Actor that tracks Shopify storefront product catalogues across many stores to extract and compare product and variant-level metadata and to detect inventory and price changes. It supports a full catalogue export mode that enumerates every product variant with pricing, compare-at price, computed discount, availability status, SKU, vendor, product type, tags, image links and product links; and a monitor mode that performs scheduled checks, establishes a baseline on first run, and thereafter emits only detected changes such as new products or variants, price decreases and increases, items returning to stock, items becoming sold out, and removed products. The Actor fetches the public product catalogue endpoints that Shopify sites publish, respects robots.txt and skips stores that disallow access or respond with access errors, paces requests per store, and produces structured change records including prior vs. current price and availability and detection timestamps. It also supports filtering by keywords, product type, vendor, price range, stock state and discount for focused exports or monitoring. ## Use cases - Export complete product and variant catalogues from multiple Shopify stores for competitor/product inventory analysis. - Schedule recurring monitoring to detect price changes (drops or increases) across tracked stores for repricing or deal discovery. - Detect and track new products and new variants as they appear on competitor or marketplace stores. - Receive alerts on stock state changes (back-in-stock, sold out) for inventory tracking and sourcing. - Identify removed or discontinued products across a set of stores for assortment and market coverage analysis. - Market research and category reporting across vendors and product types for agencies and brands. - Discovery of discounted items and opportunities for resellers, deal hunters, and category analysts.
