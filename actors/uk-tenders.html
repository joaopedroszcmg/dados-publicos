## UK Public Tenders & Contract Awards (Find a Tender) An Actor that harvests UK public procurement notices from the official Find a Tender OCDS API and produces clean, flat JSON/CSV/Excel outputs for open tenders, pipeline (planning) opportunities and contract awards. It covers above- and below-threshold notices under the Procurement Act 2023 across central government, local authorities, the NHS, universities, housing associations and utilities in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The Actor supports keyword and CPV code matching (hierarchical CPV), regional filtering using ITL1 codes, value thresholds and deadline filtering, SME/VCSE suitability filtering, lot-level metadata, and a look-back window for historical scans. Outputs provide structured notice metadata (type, title, buyer and buyer type, location and CPV classifications), procurement procedure and legal basis, estimated or awarded monetary values with currency, publication and deadline timestamps and time-to-deadline, lot counts and SME suitability flags, submission links and links to the official notice; contract award records include winning supplier details with supplier size flags and awarded contract values and dates. It merges amended notices to present the latest version and supports a monitor mode to schedule recurring runs that return only previously unseen notices. Data source: Find a Tender OCDS API (Open Government Licence v3.0). ## Use cases - Discover and shortlist open tender opportunities to bid for (target by keywords, CPV, region, contract value, deadline, SME suitability). - Spot pipeline/planning notices to prepare resourcing and early engagement before tenders publish. - Competitor intelligence, market sizing and sales lead generation from contract awards and winning-supplier data. - Scheduled monitoring to receive only new notices for a product line, client or portfolio. - Historical research and analysis of awards and notices within a configurable look-back window (up to 365 days).

Fonte dos dados: https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/api/1.0/ocdsReleasePackages.
