Drug registrations from Anvisa's official open data: product, active ingredients, holding company, therapeutic class, category, status and expiry. Monitor mode returns only new, deactivated or re-dated registrations.

What you get

  • Registration and process number
  • Product
  • Active ingredients
  • Holding company (CNPJ)
  • Therapeutic class and category
  • Status and expiry

What it's for

  • Track a competitor's registrations by company ID
  • Map generics and similars for an active ingredient
  • Alerts for registrations about to expire

{
  "termos": [
    "losartana"
  ],
  "maxResults": 20
