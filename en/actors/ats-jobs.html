Give it companies (career site link or ats:company) and get open jobs in one schema, with salary when published, filters for keyword, location and remote, and a monitor mode that reports new and closed jobs.

What you get

  • Company
  • Job title
  • Department
  • Location and remote
  • Salary (when published)
  • Posted date
  • Status (open/closed)
  • Job link

What it's for

  • Job feed from target companies for recruiting
  • Hiring signals for B2B prospecting
  • Salary research by role and company

Example input

{
  "companies": [
    "greenhouse:stripe",
    "https://jobs.lever.co/spotify",
    "https://careers.smartrecruiters.com/BoschGroup"
  ],
  "maxJobsPerCompany": 10,
  "maxResults": 30
}
