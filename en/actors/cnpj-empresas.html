Send thousands of CNPJ numbers and get legal name, registration status, activity code (CNAE), address, size and share capital. Invalid or unknown numbers are free. No personal data of partners.

What you get

  • CNPJ and legal name
  • Trade name
  • Registration status
  • Main activity (CNAE)
  • Company size
  • City and state
  • Opening date

What it's for

  • Enrich a customer or supplier list
  • Check registration status before a deal
  • Segment companies by activity code and size
