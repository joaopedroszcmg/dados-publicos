Reads the public catalogue of Shopify stores: products, variants, price, compare-at price, discount and availability. Takes a whole store or a single collection (/collections/<handle> URL). In monitor mode it returns only changes: new products, price drops or rises, back in stock and sold out.

What you get

  • Store
  • Collection
  • Product and variant
  • Price and compare-at price
  • Discount
  • Currency
  • Availability
  • Product link

What it's for

  • Track competitor prices
  • Back-in-stock alerts
  • Track a single collection (e.g. sale or new arrivals)
  • Export a store's catalogue to a spreadsheet

Example input

{
  "stores": [
    "www.allbirds.com",
    "www.kyliecosmetics.com"
  ],
  "maxProductsPerStore": 50,
  "maxResults": 45
}
