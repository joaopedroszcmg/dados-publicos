How to get Brazil's open IT tenders every day (no code)

The problem

Brazil's national public procurement portal (PNCP) lists tenders from every level of government: federal, state and municipal. Finding the ones that matter to your company every day is slow: the portal's search is manual, it doesn't tell you when something new appears, and it won't give you the line items in a spreadsheet.

The solution in five minutes

Brazil open tenders (PNCP) searches open calls by keyword, state and procurement method, and can return only the calls you haven't seen yet.

  1. Open the Actor on Apify Store and click Try for free.
  2. Paste this input (electronic reverse auctions for IT in São Paulo, new calls only):
{ "keywords": ["notebook", "computador", "servidor"], "ufs": ["SP"], "modalidades": ["6"], "onlyNew": true, "monitorId": "it-sp", "maxResults": 100 }
  1. Run it once to create the baseline. Then schedule one run a day in Schedules.
  2. In Integrations, send the results to email, Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook.

Every day you get only the new calls, with buyer, description, estimated value, bid deadline and the link to the notice. Keywords are in Portuguese because the notices are.

Useful variations

  • Direct purchases closing within 7 days: "modalidades": ["8"], "closingWithinDays": 7.
  • Line items and unit prices: "includeDetails": true adds each item with quantity, value and small-business exclusivity.
  • Large works only: "minValue": 1000000 with "modalidades": ["4", "5"].
  • Several monitors: use a different monitorId per client, segment or region.

What it costs

Pay per use: each delivered notice is charged once (prices on the Actor's Pricing tab). Repeated notices in monitor mode are free. You can cap the spend per run.

Limits

Data comes from PNCP's public APIs. When the portal is slow, runs take longer. The Actor doesn't submit bids or access the bidding systems: it finds the calls and takes you to them.
